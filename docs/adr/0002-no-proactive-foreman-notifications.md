# No proactive status notifications to foremen in v1

Foremen learn status by calling the assistant (status lookup) or from a person telling them the outcome; the system sends no SMS/push updates to foremen. We parked notifications to keep the pilot focused on capture and resolution. Accountability for closing the loop moves to the office: a resolved request not marked "foreman informed" within 2 business hours is flagged Uninformed on the dashboard.
